# 20. Group types, restorable deletes, covers and shared notes

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Splitwise lets a group be a home, a trip, a couple or something else, lets anyone undo a deleted group, shows a cover photo and has a shared whiteboard. Potluck's `delete_group` removed the row and everything under it at once, `groups.cover_path` had no upload anywhere, and categories were twelve flat ids. Several other branches change the same screens and functions at the same time (settle up, recurring, search and insights, guest links, export and import, placeholder members), so every change here has to stay additive and small where it touches shared code.

## Decision

- **Type and trip dates.** `groups.type` (enum `group_type`: `home`, `trip`, `couple`, `other`, default `other`) plus `starts_on` and `ends_on` (check: end on or after start). Mostly a label, as in Splitwise: trips show their dates, homes offer rent and utilities as monthly repeating expenses on an empty expenses tab. New RPC `create_group_v2` takes them; `create_group` stays for older clients. Admins change them with the same direct update as the other settings (column grants).
- **Deleting is undoable for 30 days.** `delete_group` keeps its signature, owner check and settled-balances check, but only stamps `deleted_at` and `deleted_by`, so old clients keep working and simply get a group that can come back. Hiding happens in one place: `private.my_group_ids`, `is_group_member`, `is_group_admin`, `group_role` and `require_member` leave deleted groups out (`require_member` raises `group_deleted`), so every policy and every RPC treats the group as gone without being redefined. Any member restores it (`restore_group`), as in Splitwise; `my_deleted_groups()` feeds "Recently deleted" on the Groups page. The nightly `purge-deleted-groups` job removes groups deleted more than 30 days ago. Core `daysLeftToRestore` counts the same 30 days.
- **Nothing lands in a deleted group.** Rather than redefine `materialize_recurring_expenses`, `accept_invite` and `send_event_reminders` (bodies other branches are changing), triggers do it: inserting an expense or a membership raises `group_deleted` (the recurring job's per template handler skips it and catches up after a restore), notifications whose `data.group_id` is a deleted group are dropped, and `delete_group` revokes the open invite links with the delete timestamp so `restore_group` reopens exactly those.
- **Everywhere else.** Helpers that join `group_members` themselves (`my_event_ids`, `can_manage_event`), `my_timeline`, `monthly_summary` and the `ics-feed` function check `deleted_at` too (`20261113213127`). Guest links close with the group and reopen on restore, backups leave it out, and imports into it are refused. The other members get a `group.deleted` notification that opens the deleted group's page with Restore, the feed keeps the delete line, and restoring notifies `group.restored`.
- **Settled means no debts at all.** With simplify off, debts can run in a circle (each owes the next) while every net is zero, so deleting also needs `private.group_debts` to be empty, and nothing (`friend_balances`, `settle_with_friend`, a payment insert) reaches a deleted group.
- **One-on-one tabs cannot be deleted** (`direct_group_not_deletable`), as in Splitwise: `get_or_create_direct_group` would otherwise keep returning a tab nobody can see.
- **Covers** live in the existing public `group-covers` bucket under `<group_id>/`. Writes stay owner and admin only through the existing policies (which now refuse deleted groups through `is_group_admin`) under the two-step policy of [ADR 13](0013-two-step-sign-in-enforced-in-the-database.md). The anon "public read" listing policy is replaced by a members-only one: the public URL still serves the image to the join page, but outsiders can no longer list every group's folder. The client shrinks photos to 1600px WebP, checks the 10 MB limit on the result, and gives each upload a new name; `groups_cover_path_check` keeps `cover_path` under the group's folder.
- **Shared notes** are a table, `group_whiteboards` (one row per group, body up to 10000 characters, `version`), not columns on `groups`, because `groups` is not in the realtime publication and the notes must update live. Writes go through `save_group_whiteboard(group_id, body, base_version)`, which refuses a stale `base_version` (`whiteboard_conflict`) instead of silently overwriting; the editor saves after a pause in typing and lets the person keep their text or take the newer one. The log is the group's activity: `group.notes_updated`, one line per person per ten minutes, with a short excerpt.
- **Subcategories** are stored as their own ids in `expenses.category` (`taxi`, not `transport.taxi`), unique across both levels, so every stored value stays valid and the column needs no change. `parentCategory()` in core rolls them up. **Suggestions** (`suggestCategory` in core) try the group's own history first (the same description used before, newest first), then a multilingual keyword list (longest phrase wins: "Uber Eats" is takeout, "Uber" a taxi), then a distinctive word shared with an earlier description. The form follows the suggestion until someone taps a category.
- QR invites are drawn on the client from the existing link (`uqr`, no server change).

## Consequences

- Anything reading `group_members` or `groups` directly, without the helpers, still sees deleted groups. New code must use `my_group_ids` / `require_member`, and any new system job that writes into a group should expect `group_deleted`.
- The recurring job retries a deleted group's due templates every night until it is restored or purged; after a restore it catches up missed periods (at most 12 per run).
- Storage files of a purged group (covers, receipts) are not removed, as before for hard deletes.
- Totals and charts that group by `expenses.category` (search and insights) should roll subcategories up with `parentCategory()`, or show them as their own rows on purpose.
- Notes have no per-version history beyond the activity excerpts. Two people typing at once is settled by choice, not merged.
