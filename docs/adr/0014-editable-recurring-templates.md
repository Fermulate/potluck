# 14. Editable repeating expenses

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

A repeating expense is a row in `recurring_expenses` (a template) that a nightly job copies into a new expense on each due date (00:10 UTC). Until now a template could only be paused or removed: changing rent from 1500 to 1600 meant stopping it and adding it again. Splitwise lets people change the amount and split of a repeating expense for the copies still to come, offers every 2 weeks, makes an existing expense repeat, and skips a single occurrence. Batch 1 ([ADR 12](0012-several-payers-refunds-and-stored-items.md)) added several payers, adjustments and stored line items to expenses, and templates already keep `payers` in the same shape; a template did not keep the items of a by-item bill or its notes, so a posted copy lost them.

## Decision

- **A template is a recipe for future expenses only.** `update_recurring_expense(id, payload)` takes the `create_expense_v2` payload (description, amount, currency, payers, split, items, tax, tip, discount, category, notes) plus `recurrence` and `next_run_on`, validates it with the same helpers (`private.compute_split_rows`, `private.itemized_splits`, the payers checks) and rewrites the template. Expenses that already posted are separate rows and never change.
- **Who may change it follows the expense rule.** An admin, or anyone the template involves: whoever set it up, a payer, or someone in the split (`private.recurring_involves`, used by the RPCs and by the update and delete policies, so pausing and stopping follow the same rule). Every other change goes through `update_recurring_expense`: the only direct column grant left is `active` (pause and resume), so nobody can backdate `next_run_on` around the RPC's checks. Members it does not involve see a read-only summary with who pays and how it splits.
- **Templates store a by-item bill and notes as entered.** New columns `items`, `tax_cents`, `tip_cents`, `discount_cents`, `notes`; `splits` keeps the shares worked out from the items, so the job still posts exact shares, and each posted copy gets the items and the notes.
- **The repeat day is stored and kept.** `anchor_day` is the day of month a monthly or yearly template repeats on. It is set when a template is created or someone picks a new next date. Changing only the interval keeps it, so a 31st stays the 31st after a short month. Switching from weekly or every 2 weeks to monthly or yearly takes the day from the next date instead (a weekly series has no day of the month of its own): weekly next on Thursday the 12th becomes monthly on the 12th. Older templates fall back to the day of their first posted expense.
- **Every 2 weeks is an interval value.** `recurrence` gains `biweekly` (14 days), in `private.recurring_step` and core `nextOccurrence` with the same cases.
- **"Today" is the person's own day.** The next date may not be before the caller's today, and making an expense repeat starts from the caller's today, both read from `profiles.timezone` (`private.local_today`), which is the day the app shows them. A zone Postgres does not know (the app stores what the device reports) counts as UTC (`private.user_time_zone`), here and for the heads-up. A next date that is today in UTC-12 but yesterday in UTC posts on the next nightly run.
- **Making an expense repeat does not back-charge.** `make_expense_recurring(expense_id, recurrence)` copies the expense's amount, payers, split, items and notes into a template and links the expense to it. The first copy is one period after the expense's date, or, if that is already past, the first date still to come (`private.recurring_next_from`, core `firstOccurrenceFrom`). Creating a backdated repeating expense from the form still catches up, as before.
- **Skipping moves the date.** `skip_recurring_expense(id)` steps `next_run_on` one period on; nothing is stored about the skipped date except an activity entry. The app offers it on the group's list, the template and each posted copy, and a skip while the editor is open keeps the edits in it.
- **Departed members.** A template that names someone who left (or closed their account) cannot post. It pauses as soon as that person leaves, is removed or closes their account (trigger `group_members_pause_recurring` on the membership row, and `delete_my_account` for groups whose membership stays because of a balance, both through `private.pause_recurring_naming`): it logs `recurring.paused` with who left and sends `recurring.paused` to whoever set it up (or the owner and admins, if they are gone too). The job does the same when a template still naming someone gone comes due, as a backstop. It cannot be resumed (`split_member_not_in_group` from a trigger; the app disables Resume and says to take that person out), and its money cannot change unless the change takes that person out. Its details stay editable. An expense naming someone who left cannot start repeating.
- **Heads-up at 9 in the morning, local time, the day before.** `private.send_recurring_heads_ups(run_at)` runs twice an hour (cron `recurring-heads-up`, :05 and :35, so half-hour zones such as India hear at about 09:05 too) and sends `recurring.upcoming` to everyone a template names (payers and people with a share), each with their own share. A person is due at 09:00 on their local day before the posting date (`private.user_time_zone`); if that moment already passed when the template was set up or moved, they hear on the next run, but never at or after 00:10 UTC on the posting date, when the copy posts. A late one that would land between 22:00 and 07:00 their time is marked quiet (`data.quiet`) and goes to the inbox only: `private.route_notification` gives quiet notifications no push and no email. `private.recurring_heads_ups (recurring_id, posts_on, user_id)` makes each person hear once per occurrence. The payload carries the posting date, the deadline (00:10 UTC) and the person's zone, so the inbox, push and email name the actual date and the deadline in the reader's time, never "tomorrow". The type maps to the Reminders setting ([ADR 11](0011-notification-prefs-and-email.md)), so each person turns heads-ups off for themselves; there is no per-template switch, which anyone involved could flip for everyone else. `recurring.paused` maps to edits and deletions.
- **Edits are visible.** `recurring.created`, `recurring.updated`, `recurring.skipped` and `recurring.paused` go to the group's activity.

## Consequences

- One editor: the web app opens a template in the expense form (`mode: 'recurring'`), so payers, adjustments, items and notes behave exactly as for an expense.
- A template can say something a posted copy disagrees with (the template moved to 1600, last month's copy says 1500). That is the point, and the form says so; the expense's own history covers each copy.
- Skipping leaves no record on the template, so "undo skip" is changing the next date back by hand.
- The heads-up job runs 48 times a day and keeps one small row per person and occurrence. Zones with 45-minute offsets (Nepal, Chatham) hear at about 09:20. Someone in UTC-12 hears late on the day before in UTC terms, still before the post. A late heads-up at night is only in the inbox, so someone may not see it before the copy posts; that is the trade for not waking them.
- Widening who may edit from "creator or admin" to "anyone involved" means a person in the split can change or stop rent someone else set up, as they already can with an expense they paid. Every change is logged.
- The `recurrence` enum and the template columns are additive; old clients ignore them, and a biweekly template shows as an unknown interval in an old client until it updates.
